# How it works

Sipquel runs on an ESP32-P4 touchscreen and talks to your gear over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.

## 1. Dose

Put the beans on your scale. Sipquel reads the weight live and settles on your dose, or you confirm the weight yourself.

## 2. Grind

Sipquel sets the grinder to the recipe's setting, then times the grind and records the setting actually used.

## 3. Brew

- **Espresso:** pressure, pump flow and cup weight are drawn as the shot runs. Open the details to pin any moment.
- **Pour-over:** each step shows its target water and time, with the weight live from your scale.

## 4. Remember

When the brew ends it goes into your journal with every number and, for espresso, its curve. Rate it, add a note, and next time pull it again with the same settings.

## On your phone

On the same Wi-Fi, open `sipquel.local` in your phone's browser for the same bench and the same journal.
