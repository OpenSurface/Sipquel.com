# FAQ

## When can I get it?

The beta opens soon. [Join the Sipquel Discord](https://discord.gg/mCAuV6bRP) to be among the first.

## Do I need a phone or an account?

No. Sipquel works on its own screen. Your phone is optional: open `sipquel.local` on the same Wi-Fi to use it as a second screen. There is no account and no subscription.

## Where is my data kept?

On the panel, optionally on its SD card. Nothing is sent to a cloud.

## Which coffee gear does it work with?

See [supported devices](/guide/devices). Your gear isn't listed? Tell us in the beta.

## Does it control my machine?

Sipquel reads your machine live and prepares it from a recipe or a past brew. Brewing itself stays in your hands: Sipquel never pulls a shot or heats water on its own.

## Is it open source?

Not yet. The firmware is private during the beta.
