# Join the beta

The Sipquel beta is almost ready. Beta testers get the firmware early, help find what needs fixing, and shape what comes next.

## Who it's for

- You brew espresso or pour-over at home.
- You own at least one of the [supported devices](/guide/devices), ideally a BOOKOO scale.
- You don't mind reporting what you find.

## How to join

The beta is organised in the Sipquel Slack. The invite link will be posted here.
