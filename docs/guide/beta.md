# Join the beta

The Sipquel beta is almost ready. Beta testers get the firmware early, help find what needs fixing, and shape what comes next.

## Who it's for

- You brew espresso or pour-over at home.
- You own at least one of the [supported devices](/guide/devices), ideally a BOOKOO scale.
- You don't mind reporting what you find.

## How to join

The beta runs on the **Sipquel Discord**. Join the server, say hello and tell us what is on your bench. Beta builds, release notes and bug reports all live there.

<BetaButton label="Join the beta on Discord" discord />

## What you'll need

- A 7-inch ESP32-P4 touchscreen panel (see [Hardware](/guide/hardware)).
- At least one supported scale, grinder or machine.
