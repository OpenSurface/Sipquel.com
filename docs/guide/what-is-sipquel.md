# What Sipquel is

Sipquel is a small touchscreen for your coffee bar. It sits next to the grinder, connects to the gear you already own, and turns every brew into a record you can learn from.

## What it does

- **Follows the whole brew.** Dose from the scale, grind from the grinder, the shot from the machine or each pour from the scale, all live on one screen.
- **Keeps your journal for you.** Every shot and pour is saved with its numbers: dose, grind setting, time, yield, ratio and the extraction curve.
- **Helps you dial in.** Rate a brew, note how it tasted (sour to bitter, acidity, sweetness, body), and compare it with the ones before.
- **Brings a good brew back.** Pick a recipe or a past brew, and Sipquel sets the grinder and gets the machine ready the same way.
- **Guides pour-over.** Bloom, pour and swirl step by step, with the target water and the weight live from your scale.

## What it isn't

- It isn't a phone app. The panel is the instrument; your phone is a second screen when you want one.
- It isn't a cloud service. There is no account and no subscription, and your coffee data stays on the panel.
