# Hardware

Sipquel runs on an **ESP32-P4** touchscreen panel:

| Panel | Resolution |
|---|---|
| 7-inch | 1024 × 600 |
| 4-inch | 800 × 480 |

It connects to your scale, grinder and kettle over Bluetooth, and to your espresso machine and your phone over Wi-Fi. Coffee data can be kept on an SD card in the panel.

Which panels and where to buy them will be listed here when the beta opens.
